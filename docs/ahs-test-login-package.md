# HazardOS — AHS Evaluation Access

Here's everything you need to get into HazardOS and have a look around.

**Web address:** https://hazardos.app

The app works in any modern browser, on desktop and on a phone. There's nothing to install, though on a phone you can add it to your home screen and it'll behave like a normal app (useful if you want to try the field side of things).

## Before you start

This is a sandbox, not your live system. It's loaded with a fictional abatement company's data so there's something to look at on every screen. Poke at it however you like, change things, delete things, break things. We rebuild it with one command.

Two things to know:

- **Please don't enter real customer information.** Names, addresses, phone numbers, anything from an actual job. This environment gets deleted once you're done with it, and we'd rather your real data went straight into your own system instead.
- **Nothing you do here carries over.** When you're ready to start working for real, we'll set up your own organisation from scratch with your branding, your team, your pricing.

## What's already in there

## Worth looking at

If you've only got twenty minutes, these are the parts we'd most like your opinion on:

**The CRM.** It's the first tab and it's meant to be where your day starts. Contacts, companies, opportunities, and a drag-and-drop pipeline board.

**A survey through to an invoice.** Open a completed job and work backwards through the estimate and the survey it came from. The question we care about is whether that chain matches how you actually quote work.

**The phone experience.** Sign in as a technician on your mobile and open a site survey. This is the piece that's hardest to get right and where your field staff's opinion matters more than ours.

**Anything that feels wrong.** Terminology that isn't what you'd say, steps in an order that doesn't match your process, a field you'd never fill in, something obviously missing. That's the most useful feedback there is and it's much cheaper to change now than later.
